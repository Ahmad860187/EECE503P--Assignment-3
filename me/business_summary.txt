Clean Break
You handle the feelings. We handle the boxes.

Clean Break is a small moving and courier company in Beirut with one pretty specific job: we sort out the practical side of a breakup. If you lived with someone and it ended, you know the list. Somebody has to move out, your stuff is at their place, their stuff is at yours, and there's a box of photos nobody wants to deal with. We take care of it, so you don't have to see your ex just to get your charger back. That's really our whole mission: making the end of a relationship a little less painful by taking the awkward errands off your hands.

We do four things. Silent Move-Out: you leave for the afternoon, we pack and move everything, and it's done in under 4 hours ($150 for a studio or 1-bedroom, $220 for 2 bedrooms, $300 for 3 or more). The Swap: one of our couriers takes your things to your ex and brings theirs back, using a photo checklist you've both approved. It's $40 a trip in Beirut and $55 to Metn, and it only happens if both of you agree. Memory Vault: we keep the sentimental stuff in a sealed box for 3, 6 or 12 months, and when time's up you decide whether to take it back, send it to your ex (only if they want it) or donate it. That's $15 a month per box, or $150 for a full year. Fresh Start: a deep clean, the furniture moved around and three new plants, so the place stops feeling like "ours" ($120 for a studio or 1-bedroom, $170 for anything bigger). If you want the move-out, one swap and 3 months of storage together, the Full Clean Break bundle is $210 (studio or 1-bedroom only).

Ahmad Karnib, Mohamad Hamdan and Zakaria El Labban started Clean Break in 2024, after spending a weekend helping a friend move out of a shared flat. It took two days, a lot of awkward silence and, in the end, two months to get one PlayStation back. Ahmad runs the company, Mohamad runs the movers and the storage, and Zakaria looks after our clients.

We're in Mar Mikhael and we cover Beirut and Metn. We work Monday to Saturday, 9 to 7, and we need 48 hours' notice. If you need us sooner than 24 hours it's 50% extra. You book with a 30% deposit and can pay in cash (USD), by Whish or by card. Why people pick us: you never have to see your ex, we don't take sides, a full move-out takes under 4 hours, and our vans have no logo on them. A few things we don't bend on: we carry belongings, never messages. If you both want the same item, it stays where it is until you agree in writing, because we're movers, not lawyers. And if you feel unsafe, please don't book us. Call 112.
